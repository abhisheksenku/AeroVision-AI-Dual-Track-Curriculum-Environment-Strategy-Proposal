# OSHA Ground Facility Surface Traffic Safety Standards

## Section 1: Surface Speed Limit Mandates
- **Statute Reference**: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178(n)(8) Powered Industrial Trucks
- **Requirement**: Under all travel conditions, vehicles and industrial trucks must be operated at a speed that will permit them to be brought to a stop in a safe manner. In indoor facilities and mixed pedestrian-vehicle corridors, surface speed limits are strictly capped at 15 mph (24 km/h); external perimeter access roads are capped at 31 mph (50 km/h).
- **Enforcement**: Any vehicle exceeding 50.0 km/h on external roadways or 25.0 km/h in mixed zones must be recorded as an OSHA speed infraction.

## Section 2: Pedestrian Right-of-Way and Separation
- **Statute Reference**: OSHA 29 CFR 1910.176 & ANSI/ITSDF B56.1
- **Requirement**: Pedestrians always maintain absolute right-of-way in shared walkways. Industrial vehicles must slow down, sound audible horns at cross aisles, and maintain a minimum physical safety buffer of 2.0 meters from any detected pedestrian.
- **Enforcement**: Trajectory proximity analysis calculating inter-object distance $< 2.0\text{ meters}$ between a vehicle track and a pedestrian track must trigger an immediate collision avoidance warning.

## Section 3: Directional Flow and One-Way Lane Compliance
- **Statute Reference**: OSHA General Duty Clause Section 5(a)(1)
- **Requirement**: In defined one-way logistics arteries, vehicles must strictly adhere to posted travel directions. Counter-flow driving creates high-consequence head-on collision risks and is strictly prohibited.
- **Enforcement**: Bidirectional tripwires calculating inward vs outward dot-product vector alignments must flag counter-flow direction violations.
