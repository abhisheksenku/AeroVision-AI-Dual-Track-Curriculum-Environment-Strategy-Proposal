# FAA Part 107 Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems Regulations

## Section 1: Visual Line of Sight and Separation
- **Statute Reference**: 14 CFR § 107.31
- **Requirement**: The remote pilot in command, the visual observer, and the flight controls must maintain continuous visual line-of-sight (VLOS) with the unmanned aircraft throughout the entirety of flight operations without aid other than corrective lenses.
- **Enforcement**: Any drone video tracking system operating beyond visual range must be paired with radar or ground detect-and-avoid (DAA) telemetry.

## Section 2: Operations Over Human Beings and Moving Vehicles
- **Statute Reference**: 14 CFR § 107.39 & § 107.145
- **Requirement**: No remote pilot may operate a small unmanned aircraft over human beings unless that human being is directly participating in the operation, located under a covered structure, or inside a stationary vehicle providing reasonable protection from falling debris.
- **Enforcement**: Aerial cameras detecting human pedestrian clusters within 15 meters of an active flight path must issue immediate proximity alerts and execute lateral deviation.

## Section 3: Operating Limitations and Groundspeed
- **Statute Reference**: 14 CFR § 107.51
- **Requirement**: The groundspeed of the small unmanned aircraft may not exceed 87 knots (100 miles per hour / 160 kilometers per hour). The altitude of the small unmanned aircraft cannot be higher than 400 feet above ground level (AGL).
- **Enforcement**: Automated kinematic estimators calculating instantaneous speeds exceeding 160 km/h must trigger critical airworthiness alerts.

## Section 4: Hazardous and Reckless Operation
- **Statute Reference**: 14 CFR § 107.23
- **Requirement**: No person may operate a small unmanned aircraft system in a careless or reckless manner so as to endanger the life or property of another.
- **Enforcement**: Rapid deceleration, uncontrolled yawing, or aggressive proximity to perimeter structures constitutes reckless operation under federal oversight.
